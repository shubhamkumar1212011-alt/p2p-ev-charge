How ChargeShare Works

Verified Bays, PIN Relay & Charge-and-Chill.

1. EV-Specific Estimator

Drivers select their car (Nexon.ev, MG ZS EV, Tiago.ev) to see exact 20%→80% charging time and ₹ tariff before booking.

2. 6-Digit Hardware PIN

Power relay unlocks strictly when the driver enters their time-bound 6-digit booking PIN at the host unit.

3. Razorpay Escrow

100% auto-refund if a bay is blocked; hosts receive automated payouts after verified sessions.

4. Charge & Chill (450m)

Live OpenStreetMap integration highlights walking-distance cafes, eateries, and restrooms.
